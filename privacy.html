Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 June 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how FreeParkingHero ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it to find parking in Göteborg. We built FreeParkingHero to need as little data as possible.

In short: your location is only used when you ask for it and stays on your device; analytics are optional and anonymous; we have no accounts, no ads, and we never sell your data.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller for FreeParkingHero is the app's publisher. For any privacy question or request, contact us at hello@theproductpeople.se. (Publisher legal name and address to be completed before release.)

2. Location data

  • FreeParkingHero requests your location only when you tap the locate button. We do not track your location in the background or continuously.
  • Your precise location is processed on your device to center the map and find nearby parking. It is not stored on our servers.
  • To fetch nearby parking, a rounded, coarse coordinate (about 4 decimal places) is sent to the City of Göteborg's Open Data parking service. It is not linked to your identity.
  • You can grant or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings.

3. Usage analytics (optional)

  • With your consent, we collect anonymous product-usage events (for example: screens viewed, features used, buttons tapped) to understand how the app is used and improve it.
  • Analytics are processed by PostHog, hosted in the EU. Events are tied to a random, app-generated identifier — not your name, email, Apple ID, or device advertising ID.
  • Analytics are off until you opt in during onboarding, and you can turn them off at any time. We do not use them for advertising or cross-app tracking.

4. What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email, or password (there are no user accounts).
  • No payment or card data — payment happens in the operator's own app (EasyPark, Aimo), not in FreeParkingHero.
  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party ad networks.

5. Third-party services & links

FreeParkingHero can hand you off to other apps and services that have their own privacy policies:

  • City of Göteborg Open Data (parking data source).
  • EasyPark and Aimo Park (payment) — opened via deep links when you choose to pay.
  • Apple Maps (directions).

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

6. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Core features (showing the map, finding parking near you): performance of the service you requested / our legitimate interest in providing it.
  • Analytics: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

7. Data retention

On-device data (such as cached parking and settings) stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Anonymous analytics events are retained by our analytics processor for a limited period for aggregate analysis.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, and restrict processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because analytics are anonymous and we hold no account data, we may be unable to identify individual records; you can still stop all analytics by disabling them in the app or deleting it. To exercise your rights, contact hello@theproductpeople.se.

9. Security

Data on your device (like the parking cache) is stored with iOS file protection, and any credentials the app uses are kept in the device Keychain. Network requests use HTTPS.

10. Children

FreeParkingHero is intended for drivers and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change and material updates will be surfaced in the app. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

This document is provided for transparency and is not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a qualified professional before publishing to the App Store.
